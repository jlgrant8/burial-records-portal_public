About this Portal

About the Portal


General

This portal provides access to burial records for Callander Cemetery, published by Stirling Council. Users can search by name, discover others recorded in the same grave, and use the location information to identify where the grave is situated within the cemetery. This is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Stirling Council.


GPS Coordinates

The GPS coordinates available on this portal are approximate and do not represent precise surveyed positions. They are provided as a guide to help users locate individual graves within the cemetery. In a small number of cases where a grave was not marked on the available lair map, a GPS location has not been provided.



Data & Sources


Source data

Burial records are sourced from Stirling Council in Scotland through its Open Data service.


Additional Information

The Council's records have been converted to CSV format and incorporated into this portal to make them searchable and easier to explore. GPS coordinates were derived using mapping and GIS tools, including ArcGIS, in conjunction with a lair map and onsite observations.


The original Stirling Council dataset did not include subplot information. Subplots shown in this portal have been independently identified by comparing the records with gravestone inscriptions through onsite investigation. These additions are not part of the original Stirling Council dataset.


Data Limitations

The information presented here may contain errors or omissions. It should not be considered a replacement for official Council records.
